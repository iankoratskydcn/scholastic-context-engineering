# Argument-mining coverage ranking

Perspective: span/component detection, relation extraction, scheme classification, implicit premises, claim/evidence linking, structured graph outputs.

Method: rank all 80 records by task fit, not dataset size. Direct annotated argument structures outrank large generic or synthetic resources. Formal proof, premise-selection, grounding, and fallacy resources remain complementary diagnostics. Ties use numeric category, paper order, paper ID. Duplicate benchmark families remain separate records and are flagged through evidence/benchmark names.

Top records: QT-SCHEMES (S09P08/S25P13), US2016G1tvWALTON, EthiX, ElecDeb60To16 extension, 741-topic argument-tree corpus, implicit-reasoning causal-link materials, Deep argumentative structures corpus, enthymeme scheme set, FOLIO, ProofWriter, LeanDojo/ReProver.
