# Benchmark classification notes: categories 11–15

- Classified 70 papers in source-artifact order: categories 11 (13), 12 (15), 13 (13), 14 (18), 15 (12).
- `introduces_benchmark` is reserved for a named/new benchmark or benchmark suite introduced by the paper.
- `introduces_dataset_or_corpus_used_as_benchmark` is used when the paper releases or extends a dataset/corpus that functions as an evaluation resource, or explicitly adopts a newly packaged corpus as the benchmark.
- `introduces_evaluation_task_or_metric_only` is used for a newly defined evaluation protocol/task without evidence of a new benchmark corpus.
- Existing benchmark/dataset use without introduction is marked `evaluates_existing_benchmark_only`; conceptual/method papers without benchmark production are `not_benchmark`.
- Counts by status: evaluates_existing_benchmark_only=35, introduces_benchmark=14, introduces_dataset_or_corpus_used_as_benchmark=6, introduces_evaluation_task_or_metric_only=6, not_benchmark=10.
- Evidence was taken from the supplied category source artifacts, which record paper-level verification against primary scholarly pages/PDFs. Thinly verified 2026 leads retain medium confidence where the source artifact flagged limited full-text inspection.
